# RBTGenius — 30-Day Organic Launch Plan

## Positioning

**Promise:** Pass-ready practice for the 2026 RBT exam: 1,100+ questions, full mock exams, flashcards, and readiness analytics in one app.

**Primary CTA:** “Download RBTGenius and start your first practice set.”

**Offer shown in content:** Free download. Pro is $19.99/month or $99.99/year. Lead with the monthly option; present annual as the best long-term value.

**Publishing cadence:** One short-form video daily. Post the same vertical edit to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Use screen recordings from the real app, captions on screen, and a calm voiceover. Keep each video between 18 and 35 seconds.

**Accuracy rules:** Never promise a guaranteed pass, never imply BACB affiliation, and do not mention AI Tutor. Use “RBT exam prep” instead of “official RBT questions.”
